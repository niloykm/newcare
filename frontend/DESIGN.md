# NewCare UI/UX Design System

Mirrored from repository root `DESIGN.md`.

- **60-30-10 Palette:** Clinical off-white `#F8FAFC` / `#FFFFFF` (dark `#0B0F14` / `#11161D`), hairline slate borders `#E2E8F0` / `#1E293B`, primary deep medical teal `#0F766E` (WCAG AA `5.47:1` with white text; hover `#115E59`).
- **Fonts:** `Inter` + `JetBrains Mono`; `tabular-nums` on all fees, rates, totals, metrics, and appointment slots.
- **Spacing & Geometry:** 4px/8px rhythm, `py-16 md:py-24` section breathing room, `rounded-lg` cards, `rounded-md` buttons/inputs, `object-cover object-top` portraits.
- **Anti-Slop:** Single clean header (5 links + 1 CTA), no stacked announcement bars, no floating role switcher widgets, no badge spam, no fake phone numbers or contradictory credentials.
